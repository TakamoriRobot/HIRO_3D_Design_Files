## 🛠️ Gen Zero 3D License & DIY Guidelines

The 3D printable prototype parts and chassis files for **H.I.R.O. Gen Zero** are released under **[CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)**. 

- **Can I print this at home or in my lab?** **Yes!** Gen Zero is designed for you to print on your desk, wire up on a breadboard, and start experimenting with 8-DOF planar kinematics.
- **Can I sell printed chassis kits or component bundles?** Not without a commercial license. 


Reference 3D models for off-the-shelf components (Raspberry Pi Pico, standard servos) included in the STEP assembly are for spatial reference and assembly preview purposes only. Original CAD sources retained by their respective creators/vendors.
